Who we are

A young agency, built for one thing: work that is done right

Stanza is a very recent agency of technical specialists: email deliverability, consent, accessibility, tracking, automation, development and infrastructure. Every specialist is vetted before their first mission, and every mission is run by Stanza from start to finish.

We started from a simple observation: companies do not want to recruit, brief and supervise a freelancer for a technical job. They want the job done, on time, with proof that it works. That is what we sell.

Our model

A guaranteed deliverable, not hours.

What we take on

Everything between your need and the finished result

Technical scoping

We translate your need into precise technical specifications before any work begins: scope, constraints, success criteria.

Execution and quality control

We run the execution and check every deliverable with tests before it reaches you. You follow each step in your client area.

The contractual guarantee

We stand behind the compliance of the work and the deadlines. If a specialist becomes unavailable, the project continues at no extra cost.

Why a managed model

We answer for the result, not for a profile

Stanza works as a managed marketplace. We do not sell profiles but results: we take on the technical scoping, the quality control and the contractual guarantee. That is where our margin goes.

For you, it means one contact, one price and one date set in advance, and a deliverable that is checked before you pay for it. For our specialists, it means missions that are ready to start, without prospecting or negotiating. Everyone gains from going through Stanza.

What it changes for you

You buy a deliverable, we handle the rest

  1. A clear need

    A fixed-price offer, or a quote built from your specification.

  2. The right expert

    We assign the specialist, or the team, suited to your project.

  3. Execution under control

    Deadlines tracked, tests run, and Stanza as your single contact.

  4. A guaranteed result

    A verified deliverable, on time, or refunded as set out in our guarantees.
