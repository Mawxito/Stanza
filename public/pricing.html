Pricing questions

Are the prices excluding VAT?

Yes. All prices on this page are shown excluding VAT and are one-off payments, with no subscription. The same goes for quotes (Atelier and multi-domain).

What is the difference between Standard, Express and Flash?

The work and the report are exactly the same; only the delivery time changes. Standard: 5 business days. Express: 48 h maximum. Flash: 24 h maximum (not available for cookie consent and server-side tracking, whose fastest option is Express). The clock starts as soon as we have the access we need, and it is guaranteed: if we are late, you get 50% of the price excl. VAT back in Standard, 100% in Express or Flash. In Express and Flash, the countdown is only paused for an urgent blocking issue reported by your expert (see below). Packs have a single price and their delivery time is set after the order. Work on quote has no fixed option: the delivery date is set in your quote, for a multi-domain request as for the Atelier.

What happens if you miss the delivery time?

You get money back. In Standard, we refund half the price excl. VAT; in Express or Flash, the full price excl. VAT. The work is still completed and delivered. The clock runs from the moment we have all the access we need, and in business days only. It does not run while an essential item is missing (access revoked, additional item requested by your expert), nor, in Express and Flash, during an urgency declared by your expert: the deadline is then pushed back by exactly the length of the pause. Packs are not covered: their delivery time is set after the order. Work on quote (multi-domain and Atelier) is not covered either: its delivery date is set in the quote.

Can the guaranteed time be paused?

Only in two cases, so there are no surprises. First, when an item we need is missing: access revoked during the work, or an additional item your expert asks for; the clock resumes as soon as you send it. Second, in Express and Flash only, when your expert declares an urgent issue that blocks the work, whether it comes from your side (access cut, decision needed) or ours (technical incident). Your expert then contacts you directly from your client area, shown as "Your assigned expert", the Stanza team is alerted and follows the conversation, and the clock stops until the issue is resolved. The deadline is then pushed back by exactly the length of the pause, and the late-delivery refund applies to this new deadline. You see the pause in real time in your client area. In Standard, an urgent issue does not pause the clock.

When is my card charged?

For the offers in the shop, Stripe only authorizes your card at checkout. The amount is captured once the acceptance gate passes and you sign off on the delivery report, within 5 business days. If the work does not pass, the authorization is released and you pay nothing. Orders on quote (Atelier and multi-domain) are charged immediately, as soon as you accept the quote, not when the order is handed over., the quote is paid when you accept it: the expert is already found and the work starts right away.

How do I follow my order?

From your client area, you see your order move from 0 to 100%: access received, configuration, tests, final report. For the shop's offers, that is also where you approve the delivery before your card is charged. Work on quote is charged when you accept the quote, and you follow it the same way.

How long do you keep my order data?

Temporary access you give us is erased as soon as the order is closed. 30 days after closing, all the order data is permanently deleted: the items you sent us and the deliverables we sent you. Download and save your reports on your side; we remind you before the deletion.

Are software licences included?

No. Subscriptions to third-party tools (consent platform, tracking server hosting, Make, Zapier, CRM, WhatsApp) are billed by their vendors. Each offer states exactly what is included and what is not. For the Atelier, the quote says so.

Why does server-side tracking require consent first?

Server-side tracking does not replace consent: it only sends the data your visitors have authorised. If you do not have a compliant consent platform yet, the Complete Pack installs it first, then wires the tracking.
