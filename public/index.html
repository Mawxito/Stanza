Cookies & consent

Consent first, on every page

A banner on its own proves nothing if your tags fire anyway. We deploy Axeptio, Cookiebot or Didomi on Shopify, Webflow or WordPress and block every non-essential script until your visitors agree.

The law does not ban cookies: it punishes fake banners and the lack of prior consent. The CNIL and its European counterparts fine website publishers directly, under Article 82 of the French Data Protection Act and the GDPR.

What a non-compliant site risks

  • Administrative fine, up toor 4% of worldwide annual turnover€20M
  • Daily penalty until compliance, up to€100,000
  • Public formal notice: the CNIL publishes formal notices, which damages your brand image.
  • Google Ads: without valid Consent Mode v2 signals, remarketing and conversion modelling are limited for your European visitors.

Free check: we list the cookies and scripts that fire before consent on your key pages and tell you whether your site needs to be brought into compliance.

Why it matters

Large or small, the rules and the stakes are the same

Inboxes, regulators and your customers do not grade on company size. What changes is what a mistake costs you.

Inboxes

Feb 2024

Since February 2024, Gmail and Yahoo require SPF, DKIM and DMARC from bulk senders, and Outlook followed in 2025. Unauthenticated mail is filtered or rejected.

Accessibility

28 June 2025

Since 28 June 2025, the European Accessibility Act applies to many online stores in the EU, micro-enterprises aside. Every blocked customer is a lost sale.

Cookies

Article 82

The CNIL and its European counterparts check websites that drop trackers without consent (Article 82 of the French Data Protection Act, GDPR), and fine organisations of every size, from global groups to small businesses.

Large companies

Dozens of tools send email and drop tags in your name. One misconfigured service is enough to send a whole domain to spam, or to put your brand in front of the regulator.

Questions, answered

What do you need from me to start?

After checkout, a short form asks for your domain, your email provider, your website platform and a way to grant temporary access to your DNS host, CMS or tools. Your specialist starts as soon as access is confirmed.

When is my card charged?

At checkout, Stripe authorizes your card. The amount is only captured once the acceptance gate passes and you sign off on the delivery report, within 7 days. If the work does not pass, the authorization is released and you are not charged.

How do I follow my order?

From your client area, you see your order move from 0 to 100%: access received, configuration, tests, final report. That is also where you approve the delivery before your card is charged.

How fast can you deliver?

Standard delivery takes 5 business days. If you are in a hurry, choose Express (48 hours maximum) or Flash (24 hours maximum) on the pricing page. Every delivery time is guaranteed: if we are late, you are refunded 50% of the price excl. VAT in Standard and 100% in Express or Flash.

Will my emails stop going to spam?

Authentication is the foundation Google, Yahoo and Microsoft now require for inbox placement. We configure and verify it end to end. Content and sending reputation also matter, and your report includes recommendations for both.

Which platforms and tools do you support?

Axeptio, Cookiebot and Didomi on Shopify, Webflow and WordPress; Google Workspace and Microsoft 365 for email; Google Tag Manager and Meta for tracking; Make or Zapier for automations. If your stack is different, contact us and we will tell you within a day whether it fits.

Do you provide legal advice?

No. Stanza handles the technical implementation. Your privacy policy and legal bases should be validated by your DPO or legal counsel, and the accessibility diagnostic is neither a full RGAA audit nor a certificate of compliance.
