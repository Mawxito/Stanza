Questions, answered

What do you need from me to start?

After checkout, a short form asks for your domain, your email provider, your website platform and a way to grant temporary access to your DNS host or CMS. Your specialist starts as soon as access is confirmed.

How does the escrow work?

You pay through Stripe at checkout. Funds are held until the acceptance gate passes and you sign off on the delivery report. If the work does not pass, it is fixed or refunded.

Will my emails stop going to spam?

Authentication is the foundation Google, Yahoo and Microsoft now require for inbox placement. We configure and verify it end to end. Content and sending reputation also matter, and your delivery report includes recommendations for both.

Which consent platforms and websites do you support?

Axeptio, Cookiebot and Didomi, on Shopify, Webflow and WordPress. If your stack is different, contact us and we will tell you within a day whether it fits a fixed package.

Do you provide legal advice?

No. Stanza handles the technical implementation of consent and script blocking. Your privacy policy and legal bases should be validated by your DPO or legal counsel.
